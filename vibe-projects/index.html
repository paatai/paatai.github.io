Vibe projects

  • Possible new impact sites on MarsOct 30, 2026 — Old and new public orbiter images of the same ground on Mars were compared pixel by pixel. Forty-nine dark spots appear between two dates and are not in the catalogue of known new craters: unconfirmed candidates for fresh meteorite impacts.
  • KnotsOct 29, 2026 — Two knots are the same if one can be moved onto the other without cutting it. Five examples where the answer is hard to guess, each with an animation in which every step is checked so that no strand passes through another.
  • Minimal surfacesOct 21, 2026 — Take any point and a small enough patch around it: that patch has the smallest area among all surfaces with the same boundary curve (a soap film on a wire). Six remarkable examples in 3D, each with a narrated animation, plus an intuitive dictionary of the terms.
  • Point-cloud clockOct 14, 2026 — Six balls of points that show the current time HH:MM:SS; each ball turns to show its digit, and you can drag a ball to spin it.
  • A–Z point-cloud sphereOct 13, 2026 — A cloud of white points inside a ball that spells every letter A to Z when viewed from different directions.
  • The longest thick curve on a sphereOct 4, 2026 — Put a rope of prescribed thickness on the unit sphere. How long can it be without overlapping itself?
  • Shortest flight around a spherical planetOct 2, 2026 — Shortest flight around a unit spherical planet from which every surface point becomes visible. Numerical candidate, generated with Grok 4.7 in Cursor.
  • Navier–Stokes equation (2D incompressible)Sep 5, 2026 — Solved live numerically on a discrete grid:

    ρ(∂u/∂t + u·∇u) = −∇p + μΔu + f, ∇·u = 0.

    The ink (the white stuff on the left) is added so you can see the motion of the fluid.
  • PercolationSep 2, 2026 — Each edge (in a square, triangular, honeycomb, etc. lattice) is kept with probability p and removed with probability 1−p. See what happens at the critical probability pc.
