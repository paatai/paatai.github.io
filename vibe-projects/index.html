Vibe projects

  • A–Z point-cloud sphere — A cloud of white points inside a ball that spells every letter A to Z when viewed from different directions.
  • The longest thick curve on a sphere — Put a rope of prescribed thickness on the unit sphere. How long can it be without overlapping itself?
  • Shortest flight around a spherical planet — Shortest flight around a unit spherical planet from which every surface point becomes visible. Numerical candidate, generated with Grok 4.7 in Cursor.
  • Navier–Stokes equation (2D incompressible) — Solved live numerically on a discrete grid:

    ρ(∂u/∂t + u·∇u) = −∇p + μΔu + f, ∇·u = 0.

    The ink (the white stuff on the left) is added so you can see the motion of the fluid.
  • Percolation — Each edge (in a square, triangular, honeycomb, etc. lattice) is kept with probability p and removed with probability 1−p. See what happens at the critical probability pc.
