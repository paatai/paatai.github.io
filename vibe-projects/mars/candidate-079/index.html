Candidate 79: Arabia Terra, 97 km west of Flammarion crater

Strong · roughly 85%

What changed. A compact, very dark spot about 55 to 60 m across appeared on smooth plains of Arabia Terra, 97 km west of Flammarion crater, between June 2022 and December 2024, in two CTX images from two orbits; it is still as dark 10 months later, in October 2025.

Why it may be a new impact. An October 2010 image, taken under the same sun as the October 2025 one, and a June 2022 image show no spot there, while a small fresh crater 300 m to the east and the pits around the site show in all four images. The aligned before-and-after difference picks out the spot and nothing else in the 2 km area, no dust-devil tracks are visible, and a round, sharp, very dark spot fits the blast zone of a small new crater.

Location
24.873° N, 46.517° E
Absent
3 October 2010 and 21 June 2022
Present
6 December 2024 and 17 October 2025
Size
Compact, very dark spot about 55 to 60 m across (58 × 58 m in the December 2024 image, 56 × 54 m in the October 2025 one)
Confidence
Strong candidate; roughly 85% that it is a new impact.
Caveat
The June 2022 image has a low sun and lines up only moderately, so the window could start as early as October 2010. A dust devil that stalled, or dust stripped by wind from a patch, cannot be ruled out, though wind seldom leaves so round and sharp a spot. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera; the nearest ends 18.6 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
