Candidate 42: Daedalia Planum lava plains

Strong · roughly 85%

What changed. A compact dark spot with a core about 40 m across and a faint halo appeared on the lava plains of Daedalia Planum between August 2008 and November 2018. It is in two CTX images from two orbits, of November 2018 and June 2025.

Why it may be a new impact. Three images from 2007 and 2008 show nothing there, while a dark feature 170 m to the west shows clearly in all three; the last, of 12 August 2008, was taken under the same sun as the June 2025 image and lines up almost perfectly, so the spot is not a shadow. A compact spot that stays unchanged for six and a half years is how the blast zone of a small new crater looks.

Caveat
Only two images show it, and it could have formed at any time in the 10 years between August 2008 and November 2018. Darker ground exposed by wind is the remaining alternative, though unlikely for a compact spot that has stayed unchanged from November 2018 to June 2025. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera; the nearest ends 6.7 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
