Candidate 59: Elysium Planitia near Cerberus Fossae, 190 km east of Zunil crater

Strong · roughly 90%

What changed. A cluster of four or five dark blobs within about 120 × 100 m, the largest about 50 m across, appeared on the plains of Elysium Planitia, 190 km east of Zunil crater, between January 2023 and April 2025, and is still there in July 2025, in two CTX images from two orbits.

Why it may be a new impact. Four earlier images, from 2014 to January 2023, show no cluster, while the small dark features around it show and line up in them; the April 2021 one was taken with the sun at the same height as in the April 2025 image. Several compact blobs appearing at once, with no dust devil track, are the usual signature of a meteoroid that broke up in the atmosphere.

Caveat
It has been seen for less than three months, so dark patches exposed by the wind are not ruled out, although several compact blobs appearing together make that unlikely. It formed after the end of InSight’s mission, so it cannot be in the list of InSight-era impacts confirmed with HiRISE by Bickel et al. (2025). It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera; the nearest ends 17.3 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
