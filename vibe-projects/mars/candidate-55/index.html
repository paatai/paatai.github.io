Candidate 55: Terrain between Lycus Sulci and Gordii Dorsum, 63 km south of Lycus Sulci

Promising · roughly 60%

What changed. A pitch-black, wedge-shaped dark mark about 90 by 25 m appeared on smooth ground between Lycus Sulci and Gordii Dorsum by June 2023 and is little changed in March 2025, in two CTX images from two orbits.

Why it may be a new impact. The only earlier image, from 2010, taken under almost the same sun, shows plain ground there. A sudden dark mark fits the blast zone of a small new impact, and an impact at a low angle can stretch it into this shape.

Location
15.819° N, 213.525° E (146.475° W)
Absent
18 May 2010
Present
2 June 2023 and 10 March 2025
Size
Wedge-shaped dark mark about 90 by 25 m
Confidence
Promising; roughly 60% that it is a new impact.
Caveat
The mark is elongated and slope streaks (small dust avalanches) occur nearby, so a fresh slope streak is the main alternative, although the mark lies on smooth ground rather than on a steep slope. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
