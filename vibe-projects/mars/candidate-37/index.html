Candidate 37: Rippled lava plains south-west of Biblis Tholus

Strong · roughly 80%

What changed. A small dark spot 25 to 40 m across appeared on wind-rippled lava plains south-west of the volcano Biblis Tholus, between May 2012 and July 2023. It is still there in April 2025.

Why it may be a new impact. A May 2012 image, taken under the same sun and lined up almost perfectly, would have shown it clearly and shows plain ground. The spot is round, spans several ripple crests and has not moved or changed shape in 21 months, so it is not a shadow or drifting sand.

Location

Confidence
Strong candidate; roughly 80% that it is a new impact.
Caveat
Dark sand gathering in the ripple troughs is the main alternative, and the spot is small and only moderately dark, so the confidence is lower until a HiRISE image is taken. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
