Candidate 47: Arabia Terra, 116 km north of Barth crater

Strong · roughly 80%

What changed. A small, compact, very dark spot 25 to 30 m across appeared on smooth ground in Arabia Terra, 116 km north of Barth crater, between January 2023 and November 2024. It is in images from November 2024 and January 2025, taken on two orbits.

Why it may be a new impact. Five earlier images, from 2010 to January 2023, show nothing there, and each of them would have shown it; the last was taken under the same sun as the November 2024 image, so the spot is not a shadow. The ground is smooth, so it is not a slope streak, and it is seen on two orbits, so it is not a camera glitch. A compact, very dark new spot is the typical mark of a small impact.

Location
9.396° N, 25.751° E
Absent
28 January 2010, 18 June 2012, 1 October 2012, 22 October 2012 and 11 January 2023
Present
14 November 2024 and 3 January 2025
Size
Small, very dark spot 25 to 30 m across
Confidence
Strong candidate; roughly 80% that it is a new impact.
Caveat
It has been followed for only seven weeks, so a short-lived dark patch made by wind is not excluded, and at about five CTX pixels across its shape is poorly resolved. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
