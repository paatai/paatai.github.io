Candidate 107: Arabia Terra plains 147 km north-west of Ramanathan crater

Promising · roughly 55%

What changed. A compact, very dark spot about 35 m across, with a smaller dark speck 100 m to the east, appeared on plains in northern Arabia Terra between March 2014 and December 2024, in one CTX image so far.

Why it may be a new impact. The only earlier image that could be measured, of March 2014, shows no spot there, and the February 2010 image shows none by eye. Both earlier images are noisy, but a spot this dark could not hide in them, and a compact, very dark spot with no tracks around it is how the blast zone of a small new crater looks.

Confidence
Promising candidate: roughly 55% likely to be a new impact.
Caveat
Only one image shows it, so an image defect cannot be fully ruled out, although the spot spans about 7 × 8 image pixels. The earlier images are noisy and striped: the dark feature nearby used as a check does not show in the March 2014 image, and the aligned before-and-after difference is noisy, with a deeper change 780 m away. No image from the high-resolution HiRISE camera covers it; the nearest ends 7.7 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
