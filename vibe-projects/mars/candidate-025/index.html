Candidate 25: Floor of Cassini crater, Arabia Terra

Strong · roughly 75%

What changed. A very dark, round spot 80 to 100 m across, with a dark tail 200 to 300 m long pointing south, appeared on the flat floor of Cassini crater between November 2008 and November 2017. It is in three CTX images from three orbits, darkest in October 2023 and fading by February 2026.

Why it may be a new impact. Two images from April and November 2008 show nothing there, while a dark feature 300 m away shows in both, so they would have shown a spot this dark. A very dark, round spot whose core is itself new, on a crater floor too flat for a slope streak, fits the blast zone of a small new crater.

Location
20.130° N, 34.206° E
Absent
2 April 2008 and 21 November 2008
Present

Size
Very dark round core 80 to 100 m across, with a tail 200 to 300 m long to the south
Confidence
Strong candidate: roughly 75% likely to be a new impact.
Caveat
A dark wind streak trailing behind a small obstacle could look like this, although that is unlikely because the round core is itself new. It could also have formed at any time in the nine years between November 2008 and November 2017. No image from the high-resolution HiRISE camera covers it; the nearest ends 24.5 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
