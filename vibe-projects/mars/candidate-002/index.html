Candidate 2: Phlegra Dorsa, eastern Elysium

Promising · roughly 60%

What changed. A compact dark spot about 70 m across appeared on plains at Phlegra Dorsa, in eastern Elysium, between June 2022 and May 2026, and is seen in one CTX image so far.

Why it may be a new impact. Five earlier images, from 2016 to 2022, show nothing there, three of them under a sun matching that of the May 2026 image. A known cluster of new impacts about 10 km away formed between 2016 and 2018, but this spot is still absent in the images from 2018 to 2022, so it is a separate, later event; a compact new dark spot fits the blast zone of a small new crater.

Caveat
Only one image shows it, and that image is noisy and was taken with the sun low in the sky, so a flaw in it cannot be fully ruled out. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera; the nearest ends 6 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
