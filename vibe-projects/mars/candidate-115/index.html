Candidate 115: Plains of the Nepenthes Mensae region, 408 km west of the InSight lander

Strong · roughly 85%

What changed. A dark spot, a dark core about 40 m across in a halo about 120 m wide, appeared on plains of the Nepenthes Mensae region, 408 km west of the InSight lander, between September 2019 and April 2023, in two CTX images from two orbits.

Why it may be a new impact. Two earlier images, from 2016 and 2019, show no spot there, the September 2019 one under almost the same sun as the April 2023 image, while a dark feature 190 m away shows in both, so they would have shown a spot this dark. A dark core in a halo, with no tracks and no other new dark mark in the surrounding 5 × 5 km, is how the blast zone of a small new crater looks.

Location
5.626° N, 128.792° E
Absent
14 August 2016 and 16 September 2019
Present
18 April 2023 and 6 October 2024
Size
Dark core about 40 m across in a halo about 120 m wide (112 × 128 m in the October 2024 image)
Confidence
Strong candidate: roughly 85% likely to be a new impact.
Caveat
Only two images show it, and the two earlier images line up only loosely with the striped October 2024 image, although the dark feature used as a check and the craters around the spot line up in both. No image from the high-resolution HiRISE camera covers it; the nearest ends 30 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
