Candidate 3: South-east of Gordii Dorsum, south of Olympus Mons

Promising · roughly 55%

What changed. A dark spot about 40 m across appears some time between 2011 and 2020. It then fades steadily, which is how impact blast zones behave as dust settles back over them.

Why it may be a new impact. It is seen at the same place in four separate images (2020, twice in 2024, 2026), so it is not a camera glitch, and it is seen under a higher sun than the 2011 image where it is absent, so it is not a shadow.

Location
0.906° N, 227.461° E
Absent
20 March 2011
Present
21 June 2020, then fainter in 2024 and in March 2026
Size
Dark spot about 40 m across
Confidence
Promising, roughly 55% that it is an impact.
Caveat
The main ordinary alternative is wind stripping dust from a small patch of this wind-carved terrain. The nearest HiRISE footprint is 38 km away and the nearest catalogued new crater is 54 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
