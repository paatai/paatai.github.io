Candidate 3: Wind-carved terrain south-east of Gordii Dorsum, south of Olympus Mons

Promising · roughly 55%

What changed. A compact dark spot about 40 m across appeared on wind-carved terrain south-east of Gordii Dorsum between March 2011 and June 2020. Four CTX images from 2020 to 2026 show it fading steadily.

Why it may be a new impact. It shows in the same place in all four later images, so it is not a flaw in one image. It also shows under a higher sun than in the March 2011 image, in which it is absent, so it is not a shadow. A compact dark spot that appears and then fades steadily fits the blast zone of a small new crater.

Location

Confidence
Promising candidate: roughly 55% likely to be a new impact.
Caveat
The sole earlier image lines up only modestly with the later ones, and the nearest dark feature shows only weakly in it, so it is less certain that this image would have shown the spot. A small change in the brightness of the ground caused by wind is also possible. No image from the high-resolution HiRISE camera covers it; the nearest ends 38 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
