Candidate 129: Wind-ridged plains south of Medusae Fossae, 183 km west of Medusae Sulci

Strong · roughly 85%

What changed. A compact dark spot about 45 m across appeared over a small wind ridge on the plains south of Medusae Fossae between April 2015 and April 2023 and is still there in July 2023, in two CTX images from two orbits.

Why it may be a new impact. Two earlier images, from 2012 and 2015, show only the ridge’s faint shadow there, the first under almost the same sun as the April 2023 image, while a dark feature 296 m away shows in the 2012 image. A compact dark spot appearing there, with no tracks around it, fits the blast zone of a small new impact.

Location
6.102° S, 197.379° E (162.621° W)
Absent
11 May 2012 and 14 April 2015
Present
26 April 2023 and 12 July 2023
Size
Compact dark spot about 45 m across with soft edges (70 × 62 m in the July 2023 image)
Confidence
Strong candidate; roughly 85% that it is a new impact.
Caveat
The two images with the spot are only 77 days apart, and it sits over a small wind ridge whose shading could mimic a spot, although the 2012 image, under almost the same sun as the April 2023 one, shows only the ridge’s faint shadow there. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera; the nearest ends 36.5 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
