Candidate 39: Dark spot with a tail on the plains north of Pavonis Mons

Promising · roughly 55%

What changed. A dark spot with a core about 85 × 130 m and a broad dark tail about 600 m long to the east-south-east appeared on the plains north of Pavonis Mons between April 2014 and January 2025. It is in one CTX image so far.

Why it may be a new impact. Two earlier images, of March 2010 and April 2014, show nothing there, while a dark feature 300 m to the east-south-east shows in both. The compact head is better explained by a small new impact, and the tail could be its dark blast deposit blown downwind.

Location
7.390° N, 246.684° E (113.316° W)
Absent
8 March 2010 and 22 April 2014
Present
25 January 2025
Size
Dark core about 85 × 130 m, with a broad dark tail about 600 m long to the east-south-east
Confidence
Promising candidate; roughly 55% that it is a new impact.
Caveat
Only one image shows it, and a dust devil or a wind streak explains the tail nearly as well, though less so the compact head. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera; the nearest ends 6.3 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
