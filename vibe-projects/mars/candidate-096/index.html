Candidate 96: Floor of Tikhonravov crater, Arabia Terra

Promising · roughly 65%

What changed. A compact dark spot about 40 m across, a dark core in a diffuse halo, appeared on the floor of Tikhonravov crater in Arabia Terra, between July 2020 and April 2025, in one CTX image so far.

Why it may be a new impact. Three earlier images, of 2010 and 2020 and all aligned, show no spot there, while a small crater about 120 m to the north-north-east shows in all four; a compact dark core in a diffuse halo is how the blast zone of a small new crater can look.

Location
12.848° N, 36.119° E
Absent
14 January 2010, 31 May 2020 and 25 July 2020
Present
4 April 2025
Size
Compact dark spot about 40 m across (38 × 36 m), a dark core in a diffuse halo about 150 m across, mostly on its south-west side
Confidence
Promising candidate: roughly 65% likely to be a new impact.
Caveat
Only one image shows it, and that image is heavily compressed, though the core and halo span many of its compression blocks. The 2020 images were taken under a lower sun, though a spot this dark would show under it, the nearby feature used as a check is only faint in the earlier images, and in the aligned before-and-after difference a broad change at the eastern edge of the 2 × 2 km area is slightly deeper than the spot. No image from the high-resolution HiRISE camera covers it; the nearest ends 22.1 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
