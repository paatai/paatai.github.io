Candidate 51: Wind-ridged plains of southern Amazonis Planitia, north-east of Nicholson crater

Promising · roughly 65%

What changed. A cluster of three dark spots, each about 40 to 50 m across and all within about 180 m, appeared on wind-ridged plains of southern Amazonis Planitia between November 2021 and August 2023.

Why it may be a new impact. Two earlier images, from 2008 and 2021, taken under a similar sun, show plain ground there. A sudden cluster of compact dark spots fits the blast zones of a small impactor that broke up in the atmosphere.

Location
3.821° N, 197.517° E (162.484° W)

Size
Three dark spots, each about 40 to 50 m across, within about 180 m
Confidence
Promising; roughly 65% that it is a new impact.
Caveat
Only one image shows it, so it is rated promising rather than strong. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
