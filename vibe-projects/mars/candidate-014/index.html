Candidate 14: Arabia Terra, north-west of Schroeter crater

Promising · roughly 60%

What changed. A sharp, very dark dot 30 to 35 m across appeared on the plains of Arabia Terra north-west of Schroeter crater between March 2014 and July 2025, and is seen in one CTX image so far.

Why it may be a new impact. The two earlier images, from April 2012 and March 2014, taken under much the same sun as the July 2025 image, show nothing at the spot, while a dark feature 204 m away shows in both, so they would have caught the dot; a sharp, compact dark dot appearing on plains fits the blast zone of a small new crater.

Confidence
Promising candidate: roughly 60% likely to be a new impact.
Caveat
Only one image shows it, and its data are heavily compressed. A flaw from compression or transmission is unlikely, since such flaws look like lines or blocks rather than a round dot six pixels across, but it cannot be ruled out without a second image. No image from the high-resolution HiRISE camera covers it; the nearest ends 58.9 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
