Candidate 57: Plains in Arabia Terra, 291 km west of Henry crater

Strong · roughly 90%

What changed. A cluster of at least eight compact dark spots within about 280 m, the largest about 45 m across, appeared on plains in Arabia Terra west of Henry crater by December 2023 and is still there in January 2025, in two CTX images from two orbits.

Why it may be a new impact. Two earlier images, from 2016 and 2020, the first under a similar sun, show plain ground there. A sudden cluster of compact dark spots fits the blast zones of a small impactor that broke up in the atmosphere.

Location
9.610° N, 18.599° E
Absent

Present

Size
Cluster of at least eight dark spots within about 280 m, the largest about 45 m across
Confidence
Strong candidate; roughly 90% that it is a new impact.
Caveat
No HiRISE image covers it, so the small craters that should sit at the centres of the spots have not been seen. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
