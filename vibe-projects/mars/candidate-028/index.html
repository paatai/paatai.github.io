Candidate 28: Arabia Terra, north of Schiaparelli crater

Strong · roughly 70%

What changed. A compact dark spot 40 × 60 m appeared on flat, bright plains of Arabia Terra north of Schiaparelli crater, between October 2023 and September 2024, a window of 11 months. It is in two CTX images from two orbits, of September 2024 and July 2025.

Why it may be a new impact. Two earlier images, of December 2013 and October 2023, line up well and show nothing there, and the second was taken under the same sun as the July 2025 image. The two images that show the spot have the sun in directions 57° apart, so it is not a shadow, and a compact new dark spot on flat plains is how the blast zone of a small new crater looks.

Location
10.373° N, 12.942° E
Absent
19 December 2013 and 10 October 2023
Present
7 September 2024 and 13 July 2025
Size
Compact dark spot 40 × 60 m
Confidence
Strong candidate; roughly 70% that it is a new impact.
Caveat
No comparable dark feature lies within 300 m to show that the earlier images would have caught a spot like this, and the September 2024 image is low in contrast. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera; the nearest ends 19.7 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
