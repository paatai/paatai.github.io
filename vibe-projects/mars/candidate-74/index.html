Candidate 74: Knobby lava plains in western Daedalia Planum, 84 km north-west of candidate 27

Strong · roughly 85%

What changed. A dark spot about 40 m across appeared among low knobs on lava plains in western Daedalia Planum, 84 km north-west of candidate 27, between March 2021 and December 2022 and is still there three weeks later, in two CTX images from two orbits.

Why it may be a new impact. Three earlier images from 2010 to 2021 show only small knob shadows there, although their lower sun makes shadows longer, while a dark feature 180 m away shows in all three, and a compact new dark spot on dusty lava plains is how the blast zone of a small new crater looks.

Location
18.365° S, 222.311° E (137.689° W)
Absent
14 December 2010, 20 July 2014 and 13 March 2021
Present
31 December 2022 and 22 January 2023
Size
Dark spot about 40 m across, elongated north-south
Confidence
Strong candidate; roughly 85% that it is a new impact.
Caveat
The two images with the spot are only three weeks apart, so we cannot tell how long it lasted, and the knobby ground casts many small shadows, although the spot is largest and darkest under the higher sun. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
