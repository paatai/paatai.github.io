Candidate 135: Plains in western Arabia Terra, 328 km south-east of Gill crater

Strong · roughly 80%

What changed. A compact dark spot about 50 m across, with two dark cores, appeared on smooth plains among small knobs in western Arabia Terra between October 2014 and January 2024 and is still there in March 2024 and October 2025, in three CTX images from three orbits.

Why it may be a new impact. Two earlier images, from 2011 and 2014, the second under almost the same sun as the March 2024 image, show plain ground there, while a dark feature 300 m away shows in the 2014 image, and apart from artefacts at the edges of the view, the aligned before-and-after difference is deepest at the spot. A compact dark spot with sharp cores appearing on plain ground fits the blast zone of a small new impact.

Location
12.496° N, 10.154° E
Absent
3 January 2011 and 6 October 2014
Present
17 January 2024, 29 March 2024 and 20 October 2025
Size
Compact dark spot about 50 m across, with two dark cores about 35 m apart (94 × 52 m in the March 2024 image)
Confidence
Strong candidate; roughly 80% that it is a new impact.
Caveat
The images are strongly compressed, and three of them were aimed at a sharp boundary between brighter and darker ground in the region, where the wind moves dust around, although no such boundary is seen within 6 km of the spot; these two points are why it is rated about 80% rather than 90%. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera; the nearest ends 23.3 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
