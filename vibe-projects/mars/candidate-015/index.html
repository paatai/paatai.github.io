Candidate 15: Arabia Terra, north-west of Dawes crater

Promising · roughly 45%

What changed. A large, diffuse dark oval about 250 × 150 m, with a darker core, appeared on the dusty plains of Arabia Terra north-west of Dawes crater between August 2024 and July 2025, a window of 10.5 months, and is seen in one CTX image so far.

Why it may be a new impact. All 10 earlier images, from February 2010 to August 2024, show nothing there; four of them line up well with the July 2025 image, and an oval this large would be obvious anywhere in the 2 km patch examined in each. Of the possible explanations, the blast zone of a new impact is slightly preferred.

Location
3.306° S, 33.715° E
Absent
26 February 2010, 9 April 2012, 2 July 2020, 17 February 2022, 10 May 2022, 7 January 2023, 3 October 2023, 31 October 2023, 19 June 2024 and 20 August 2024
Present
6 July 2025
Size
Large, diffuse dark oval about 250 × 150 m, with a darker core
Confidence
Promising candidate: roughly 45% likely to be a new impact.
Caveat
Only one image shows it, and dust devils are active on these plains, so it could also be a dust-devil scar, ground scoured by wind, or a change in brightness after a dust event. No image from the high-resolution HiRISE camera covers it; the nearest ends 15.1 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
