Candidate 95: Plains north of Tithonium Chasma, 58 km north-east of candidate 47

Promising · roughly 65%

What changed. A compact dark spot about 25 m across, with a dark core, appeared on cratered plains north of Tithonium Chasma, 58 km north-east of candidate 47, between July 2023 and May 2025. It is visible in one CTX image so far.

Why it may be a new impact. Four earlier images, from 2012 and 2023, all line up with the later one and show no spot there, while a small crater 276 m to the south shows clearly in every one; the last, from July 2023, was taken under the same sun as the May 2025 image. The spot is the strongest small-scale change between the earlier and later images, and a compact dark spot with a dark core is how the blast zone of a small new crater looks.

Location
1.917° S, 268.742° E (91.258° W)
Absent
6 April 2012, 23 April 2012, 27 March 2023 and 10 July 2023
Present
25 May 2025
Size
Compact dark spot about 25 m across (26 × 24 m), with a dark core
Confidence
Promising candidate: roughly 65% likely to be a new impact.
Caveat
Only one image shows it, so an image defect cannot be fully ruled out, though the spot spans several of the blocks in which that image was compressed. A dust devil that stalled is also possible, though there are no tracks near it. No image from the high-resolution HiRISE camera covers it; the nearest ends 15.3 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
