Candidate 51: Southern Arabia Terra, east of Schiaparelli crater

Strong · roughly 90%

What changed. A compact, very dark spot with a core about 55 × 65 m appeared in southern Arabia Terra east of Schiaparelli crater, between September 2010 and May 2023, and is still there in February 2025, in two CTX images from two orbits.

Why it may be a new impact. The one earlier image, from September 2010, lines up well, was taken under the same sun as the May 2023 image and shows nothing there, so the spot is not a shadow. A compact, very dark spot unchanged for 21 months is unlikely to be darker ground exposed by the wind, and is the typical mark of a small new impact.

Caveat
With only one earlier image, the window is almost 13 years long. No image from the high-resolution HiRISE camera covers it; the nearest ends 46.4 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
