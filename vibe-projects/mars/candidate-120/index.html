Candidate 120: Elysium Planitia lava plains north of Hibes Montes

Strong · roughly 85%

What changed. A compact dark spot about 50 m across, with a faint halo, appeared on the Elysium Planitia lava plains north of Hibes Montes between May 2014 and March 2023, in two CTX images from two orbits.

Why it may be a new impact. The only earlier image, of May 2014, taken under almost the same sun as the June 2023 image, shows no spot there, while a dark feature 135 m away shows clearly in it, so it would have shown a spot this dark. Two older dark-halo craters 1.5 and 2.2 km away look the same in all three images, and a compact new dark spot with a faint halo and no tracks is how the blast zone of a small new crater looks.

Location
5.164° N, 170.667° E
Absent
10 May 2014
Present
25 March 2023 and 21 June 2023
Size
Compact dark spot about 50 m across (44 × 60 m in the June 2023 image), with a faint halo
Confidence
Strong candidate; roughly 85% that it is a new impact.
Caveat
Only one earlier image covers the site, and only two images, from March and June 2023, show the spot. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera; the nearest ends 20 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
