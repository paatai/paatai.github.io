Candidate 22: Large irregular dark patch at Olympica Fossae

Promising · roughly 45%

What changed. A large, irregular dark patch 250 × 200 m, with several darker blobs, appeared on smooth Tharsis plains at Olympica Fossae between November 2024 and July 2025, a window of seven and a half months. It is in one CTX image so far.

Why it may be a new impact. Six earlier images, from 2007 to November 2024, show nothing there, including two from October and November 2024 that line up well with the July 2025 image. The darker blobs have sharp edges and there are no tracks, which slightly favours the blast zone of a cluster of small impacts over a scar left by a dust devil or ground scoured by wind.

Confidence
Promising candidate; roughly 45% that it is a new impact.
Caveat
Only one image shows it, and a scar left by a dust devil, or ground scoured by wind, remains possible. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera; the nearest ends 11.9 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
