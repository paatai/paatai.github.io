Candidate 128: Textured plains in north-western Phlegra Montes

Promising · roughly 65%

What changed. A compact dark spot about 40 m across appeared on textured plains in north-western Phlegra Montes between February 2010 and June 2023. So far it is seen in one CTX image; the interval is so long because only two CTX images cover the site.

Why it may be a new impact. The only earlier image, from 2010, taken under almost the same sun as the June 2023 image, shows plain ground there, and a dark mark 234 m away shows in it, so that image would have shown a new spot too. Subtracting the aligned earlier image from the later one shows the strongest change in the surrounding 2 × 2 km right at the spot. A compact dark spot appearing on plain ground fits the blast zone of a small new impact.

Location
43.468° N, 155.516° E
Absent

Present

Size
Compact dark spot about 40 m across (32 × 50 m in the June 2023 image)
Confidence
Promising candidate: roughly 65% likely to be a new impact.
Caveat
Only one image shows it, so it is rated promising rather than strong, and an image defect cannot be ruled out with one image. It lies inside the area, and possibly the period, covered by the published catalogue of impacts by Bickel et al. (2025), but every entry in that catalogue has a HiRISE image, so it is probably not among them. No image from the high-resolution HiRISE camera covers it; the nearest ends 9.9 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
