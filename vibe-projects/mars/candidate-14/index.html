Candidate 14: Arabia Terra, north-west of Dawes crater

Promising · roughly 45%

What changed. A large, diffuse dark oval about 250 × 150 m appeared on these dusty plains within a window of about ten months, between August 2024 and July 2025.

Why it may be a new impact. It may be the blast zone of a new impact, and three recorded new impacts lie within 31 km.

Location
3.306° S, 33.715° E
Absent
ten images from 26 February 2010 to 20 August 2024
Present
6 July 2025
Size
Large, diffuse dark oval about 250 × 150 m
Confidence
Promising; roughly 45% that it is a new impact.
Caveat
Dust devils are active here and could also scour such a patch, so it is rated promising. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
