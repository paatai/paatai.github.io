Candidate 8: Daedalia Planum, south-west of Arsia Mons

Strong · roughly 80%

What changed. A very dark, lobed spot 50 to 60 m across appeared on the lava plains of Daedalia Planum, west of Aganippe Fossa, between 2020 and 2024. It is absent in 2020 under nearly the same lighting, and it keeps the same shape in images 11 months apart.

Why it may be a new impact. Its size and darkness match the blast zones of new impacts found elsewhere on Mars.

Location
6.868° S, 229.425° E (130.575° W)
Absent
22 February 2008 and 30 November 2020
Present
8 December 2024 and 10 November 2025
Size
Very dark, lobed spot 50 to 60 m across
Confidence
Strong candidate; roughly 80% that it is a new impact.
Caveat
It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
