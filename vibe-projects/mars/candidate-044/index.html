Candidate 44: Northern Arabia Terra

Promising · roughly 45%

What changed. An irregular dark patch about 70 m across appeared at the edge of a brighter ridge in northern Arabia Terra, between October 2008 and February 2025, in two CTX images from two orbits two months apart.

Why it may be a new impact. The one earlier image, from October 2008, lines up well and shows no patch there, while a dark feature 300 m to the north shows in it. The sun is higher in the February and April 2025 images than in the October 2008 one, and a higher sun weakens shadows, so the patch is unlikely to be shading; it may be the blast zone of a small new impact.

Caveat
A new slope streak fits nearly as well: the ground is rugged, and the patch is irregular and lies beside a slope. With only one earlier image, the window is more than 16 years long. No image from the high-resolution HiRISE camera covers it; the nearest ends 15.7 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
