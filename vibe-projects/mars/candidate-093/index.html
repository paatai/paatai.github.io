Candidate 93: Eastern Daedalia Planum, 555 km south of candidate 18

Promising · roughly 65%

What changed. A compact dark spot about 75 m across, a dark core with small dark specks around it, appeared on lava plains in eastern Daedalia Planum, between December 2017 and November 2024, in one CTX image so far.

Why it may be a new impact. Three earlier images from 2007 to 2017, all aligned, show no spot there, while the bright patches around the site line up in all four images, and the aligned before-and-after difference is deepest at the spot and nowhere else in the 2 × 2 km area around it; a compact dark core with small dark specks around it fits the blast zone of a small new crater.

Location
17.351° S, 244.989° E (115.011° W)
Absent
15 August 2007, 4 April 2010 and 31 December 2017
Present
19 November 2024
Size
Compact dark spot about 75 m across (86 × 76 m), a dark core with small dark specks around it
Confidence
Promising candidate: roughly 65% likely to be a new impact.
Caveat
Only one image shows it, so an image defect cannot be fully ruled out, though the spot spans many image lines and has small specks around it. The earlier images were taken under other suns, the August 2007 one hazy from the 2007 global dust storm and the other two with a lower sun, though none shows anything as dark, and no comparably dark feature nearby can confirm they would have shown it. No image from the high-resolution HiRISE camera covers it; the nearest ends 35.4 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
