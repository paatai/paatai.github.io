Candidate 30: Elysium Planitia, south-west of Elysium Mons

Promising · roughly 55%

What changed. A compact dark dot 20 × 35 m appeared on smooth plains of Elysium Planitia, south-west of Elysium Mons, between December 2024 and August 2025, a window of eight months. It is in one CTX image so far.

Why it may be a new impact. Six earlier images, from 2014 to December 2024, show nothing there, among them one of 21 May 2014 taken under the same sun as the August 2025 image, while a dark feature 240 m away shows clearly in all five that could be measured. A compact dark dot appearing on smooth plains could be the blast zone of a small new crater.

Confidence
Promising candidate: roughly 55% likely to be a new impact.
Caveat
Only one image shows it, and the dot is drawn out along the lines of that image, so a flaw in the image cannot be fully ruled out. No image from the high-resolution HiRISE camera covers it; the nearest ends 3.7 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
