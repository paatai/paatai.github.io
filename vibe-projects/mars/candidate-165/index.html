Candidate 165: Wind-ridged plains in Daedalia Planum, 299 km east of Arimanes Rupes

Strong · roughly 85%

What changed. A compact dark spot about 35 m across appeared on wind-ridged plains in Daedalia Planum, between December 2007 and April 2024, in three CTX images from three orbits taken over nine months.

Why it may be a new impact. The two earlier images, from 2007, show plain ground there, one of them under the same sun as the first image with the spot, and a compact dark spot appearing on dusty plains and lasting is how the blast zone of a small new crater looks.

Location
10.861° S, 217.325° E (142.675° W)
Absent
16 April 2007 and 20 December 2007
Present
6 April 2024, 2 November 2024 and 8 January 2025
Size
Dark spot about 35 m across (52 × 38 m in the April 2024 image)
Confidence
Strong candidate; roughly 85% that it is a new impact.
Caveat
The two later images are blurred or noisy and align poorly by the score, no dark feature nearby can test the earlier images, and the window is sixteen years long. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera; the nearest ends 10.3 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
