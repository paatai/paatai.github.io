Candidate 152: Lava plains 295 km west of Fortuna Fossae

Strong · roughly 85%

What changed. A compact dark spot about 30 m across appeared on lava plains 295 km west of Fortuna Fossae between June 2020 and August 2022, in two CTX images from two orbits two years apart.

Why it may be a new impact. Two earlier images from 2014 and 2020 show plain ground there under a lower sun, which would lengthen any shadow, while a dark feature 300 m away shows in both, and a compact new dark spot on dusty lava plains is how the blast zone of a small new crater looks.

Location
4.508° N, 262.316° E (97.684° W)
Absent
23 July 2014 and 1 June 2020
Present
14 August 2022 and 16 August 2024
Size
Dark spot about 30 m across (32 × 26 m in the August 2022 image), smaller and fainter by August 2024 (20 × 18 m)
Confidence
Strong candidate; roughly 85% that it is a new impact.
Caveat
It is smaller and fainter in the 2024 image, which fits a blast zone fading, and where the images are compared, crater shading at the edge of the frame stands out more than the spot. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera; the nearest ends 47.2 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
