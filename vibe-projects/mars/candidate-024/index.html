Candidate 24: Northern Arabia Terra, south-west of Ismeniae Fossae

Promising · roughly 50%

What changed. A black, compact blob 30 to 40 m across appeared on knobby plains of northern Arabia Terra, south-west of Ismeniae Fossae, between January 2010 and February 2025, in two CTX images from two orbits seven months apart; in the second, of September 2025, it is smaller and fainter.

Why it may be a new impact. The one earlier image, of 3 January 2010, was taken under the same sun as the February 2025 image and shows nothing there, while a dark feature 150 m away shows in it. A compact black spot appearing on these plains could be the blast zone of a small new crater.

Caveat
There is only one earlier image, so the window spans 15 years, and by September 2025 the spot is already smaller and fainter, unusually fast fading for a blast zone. The ground is knobby, and in some images the shadows of knobs lie everywhere, which makes a small dark spot harder to single out. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera; the nearest ends 72.8 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
