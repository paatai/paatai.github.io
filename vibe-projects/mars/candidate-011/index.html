Candidate 11: Elysium Planitia, west of Albor Tholus

Strong · roughly 80%

What changed. A compact dark spot 25 to 30 m across appeared on lava plains west of Albor Tholus between July 2022 and April 2025, and is unchanged in four CTX images over seven months; a fainter companion 195 m to the north-north-west is new too.

Why it may be a new impact. The May 2014 image, taken under the same sun as the August 2025 image, and the July 2022 image show nothing there, while the craters around it line up between the images. A compact spot that stays unchanged for seven months is not a dust devil track or wind streak but looks like the blast zone of a small new crater, and with its new companion it forms a small cluster.

Location
16.884° N, 147.869° E
Absent
22 May 2014 and 28 July 2022
Present
10 April 2025, 18 July 2025, 15 August 2025 and 28 November 2025
Size
Dark spot 25 to 30 m across, plus a fainter new companion 195 m to the north-north-west
Confidence
Strong candidate; roughly 80% that it is a new impact.
Nearby
Candidate 16, 13 km to the west-north-west, appeared earlier, between May 2014 and July 2022.
Caveat
The July 2022 image, the last one without the spot, lines up less closely than the May 2014 one, and an image from May 2024 cannot tell whether the spot was there yet. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera; the nearest ends 6.2 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
