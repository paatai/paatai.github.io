Candidate 16: Elysium Planitia, 13 km west-north-west of candidate 11

Strong · roughly 80%

What changed. A compact, round dark spot 35 to 40 m across appeared on lava plains in Elysium Planitia between May 2014 and July 2022, and is unchanged in seven CTX images up to September 2025.

Why it may be a new impact. The May 2014 image, taken under the same sun as the August 2025 image, shows nothing there, although a shadow 92 m away shows in it. Since July 2022 the spot has been present under a range of sun heights and directions, so it is not a shadow, and a compact, round dark spot that lasts for years is how the blast zone of a small new crater looks.

Confidence
Strong candidate; roughly 80% that it is a new impact.
Nearby
Candidate 11, 13 km to the east-south-east, appeared later, between July 2022 and April 2025.
Caveat
Only one earlier image, from May 2014, shows the ground before it formed. It may already be in the catalogue of new impacts of Bickel et al. (2025), as its place and time window qualify, though no HiRISE image of it exists. It lies outside every footprint of the high-resolution HiRISE camera; the nearest ends 1.0 km away. Like every candidate here, it is unconfirmed: see the caveat.
