The Four Voyages of Christopher Columbus (1492–1504)

Watch the video (MP4, 82 MB)

Animated cartoon · 9:46 · narrated, with English subtitles

A 9:46-minute animated cartoon based on a true story. It follows Columbus across the Atlantic four times, from Palos in 1492 to his year stranded on Jamaica in 1503–1504, and ends with his son Diego arriving in Santo Domingo as governor in 1509.

The story is drawn from Columbus’s own diary of the first voyage and his letters, and from other historical documents: the life written by his son Ferdinand, the histories of Bartolomé de las Casas, and letters and records by people who sailed with him. The words Columbus speaks in the film are quoted from his diary and letters, apart from two short dramatized lines. Along the way it corrects some popular myths (people in 1492 knew the Earth was round) and is honest about what the voyages meant for the Taíno people, without showing violence.

Sources: Columbus, Diario de a bordo (1492–93, in the abstract by Las Casas) and letters of 1493, 1498, 1500 and 1503 (the Lettera rarissima); Ferdinand Columbus, Historie (Venice, 1571); B. de las Casas, Historia de las Indias; D. Álvarez Chanca, letter on the second voyage (1494); D. Méndez, account of the fourth voyage (1536); M. Fernández de Navarrete, Colección de los viages y descubrimientos, vol. II (1825); J. E. Olson & E. G. Bourne (eds.), The Northmen, Columbus and Cabot, 985–1503 (1906); S. E. Morison, Admiral of the Ocean Sea (1942).
