Galaxy hunt

An automated search of public sky surveys, including Gaia DR3, looked for stars that changed brightness in ways the published record does not already list. These pages show nine candidates from that search. Every rating is the search’s own: self-vetted, not reviewed by an outside expert, and nothing has been reported or submitted anywhere. On the light curves, down means fainter. Candidate 1 also has difference images, and there a blue spot means fainter.

Candidates

  • Candidate 1: red dwarf in Phoenix that faded by up to 38% for about a year from November 2016, then dipped again briefly (10%) in November 2018 Gaia DR3 6521694778536448640 · Promising
  • Candidate 2: white dwarf in Cepheus that faded by 26% for about eight months in 2015. Only Gaia was watching Gaia DR3 2212797391765189760 · Promising
  • Candidate 3: faint, distant star in Cygnus that brightened four-fold for a few weeks in 2016, once, by the same factor in every colour Gaia DR3 1861208444041823360 · Promising
  • Candidate 4: star in Sagittarius, toward the Galactic bulge, that brightened at least two-fold around the turn of 2014/2015, once Gaia DR3 4119926316171065984 · Promising
  • Candidate 5: distant star in Puppis, roughly opposite the Galactic centre, that brightened by 70% over a few months in early 2015, once Gaia DR3 5620272814897694848 · Promising
  • Candidate 6: distant star in Serpens, toward the inner Galaxy, that brightened by 60% for about two months in 2016, once Gaia DR3 4145977633638478336 · Promising
  • Candidate 7: orange dwarf in front of the Andromeda Galaxy, steady from 1983 to 2002, then 60–90% brighter between 2010 and 2020 Gaia DR3 381248613189061632 · Promising

Weaker candidates

The search kept these two as well, and rates both weak. They are listed last.

  • Candidate 8: star in Ursa Major whose Gaia wobble suggests an unseen companion of about 1.5 solar masses Gaia DR3 1121443762310292096 · Weak
  • Candidate 9: giant star in Vulpecula whose Gaia wobble suggests an unseen companion of about 4.3 solar masses Gaia DR3 2018177545831527808 · Weak

The pictures were made from public archives: Legacy Surveys, Pan-STARRS1, the NOIRLab Astro Data Archive, SkyMapper, ZTF, Gaia, WISE and NEOWISE, and the STScI Digitized Sky Survey.
