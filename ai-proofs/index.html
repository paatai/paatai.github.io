AI proofs

These are proofs I got from Grok Bots working together with Cursor agents in various experiments. Some came from several agents working continuously for 24 hours or more. I think this information might be valuable, so I decided to share it publicly rather than keep it on my computer. Some are polished and some are raw drafts; many are problems I had thought about myself before. Feel free to use them.

How to cite: P. Ivanisvili, title, AI-generated proof, month year, https://paatai.github.io/ai-proofs/#n
